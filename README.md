# Job Command

Phone-first shop desk for Top Gun Painting in Hot Springs, AR. The chrome is the shop — logo or monogram, company name, and job tickets like `TGP-2041` — not a generic platform mark. First open is a signup on the high-end dark look: the Job Command shield sits at the top of every setup step, with a Dark/Light toggle so you can switch to the white canvas before you finish. Create account, pick the trade, crew size, accounting software, then company name, address, contact, and logo. Next: how you get paid (cards, ACH, cash/check, deposits), look (Standard Dark default, plus Color Dark and Color Ink on that step), pay period, bosses, crew names/roles/rates, and a company answering line. Company → App look still changes the theme any time after signup. Speak or type every answer. Office and field then unlock with a 4-digit PIN. Office uses the PIN picked on the first signup step (if that step is skipped: the last four of the owner phone, or `1001` with no phone on file). Crew use the last four of their phone. The server checks every PIN — there is no office bypass. Local previews only: `NEXT_PUBLIC_JC_DEV_SKIP_PIN=1 npm run dev` tries the `1001` fallback for you and shows the normal gate if it does not match. The cookie stays on that phone for 30 days. A brief Job Command shield splash fades in and out on first launch from `IntroBoot`. Set `INTRO_BOOT_ENABLED` to false in `lib/intro-boot.ts` to skip it.

When setup is done, five phone pages sit on a bottom dock. Field crew only get Command, Crew, and Schedule — Pay, Company, invoices, and job-cost cues stay on the office login. A field phone opens on **Crew**: today’s directions, tasks, notes, materials, clock in/out, this week’s hours, call/text the shop, and live alerts. Office **Company** can copy or text an employee invite link (`/j/...`). First open creates a username and password, picks W-2 vs cash/alternative pay, and files tax withholdings (status and exemptions) into payroll. After that they unlock with the last four of their phone.

Swipe left or right on Command (and the job folder) to change clients. The page stays put — no sliding overlay. On an open job stage, swipe up to the next unlocked step and down to move back. Locked stages stay closed. Arrows still work. Schedule months and pay periods swipe in their own strips. Completed pipeline bars show a gold checkmark; the next open step is the gold pulsing action.
- **Command** — job tumbler. Each colored pipeline bar opens its own isolated screen: red **New Lead Call**, orange **Schedule Estimate**, yellow **Assign crew / Materials**, light green **Active on job**, dark green **Invoice Paid**, slate **Finished Archive**. Later bars stay locked until the current checklist is signed off. Completing a step hops to the next isolated color. Talk/AI sits at the top of every stage as one walkie-talkie mic. Orange also keeps a dedicated camera next to it. Hold to talk (or tap with typed notes). The co-pilot parses what you said, saves it on the phone, and tells you the exact next step in plain English — never “task complete.”
- **Crew** — office: person rolodex, clock, today’s route. Field: employee home with clock in/out, weekly hours, shop call/text, alerts, Navigate, materials, and one Street/Top map on the live stop.
- **Job folder** — AI voice/text and camera sit at the top of the open job. Customer, Bid, Work, and Invoice tabs follow. Work keeps a Crew & Schedule block on the same page: **+ Assign crew**, inline date and hour steppers, and automatic busy lockouts if that person is already on another job for overlapping hours. Those assignments write the calendar and timesheet immediately — no jump to the Schedule tab. Bid and invoice still take spoken line items.
- **Schedule** — month calendar stays. Under it, every person is a tap-to-edit row (name and hours). Jobs already covering that window are grayed Busy so you cannot double-book from the calendar either. Hold the mic: “Change Casey's hours to 8.” Saves on blur or enter, including offline.
- **Pay** — this period, week archive, year filing record
- **Company** — business, books, bosses, 30-day trial countdown, Job Command $199/mo or $1,990/yr flat-rate + AI answering subscriptions, card and bank linking, Terms & Privacy, employee invite links, and year-end books (CSV/PDF for the CPA: cash collected, billed materials, accrued material tax, and payroll wages). Active plan or open trial unlocks the itemized packets; crew clocks stay open if the trial ends.

Drive times use Google Distance Matrix when `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` (or `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY`) is set. Without a key the app still packs slots from OpenStreetMap/OSRM or a Hot Springs driving estimate, and still embeds Google Maps / Street View plus one-tap `maps/dir` navigation. Light green **Active** shows one map of the job (Street View by default, Top/satellite on toggle — no second directions iframe), live crew GPS while field phones are clocked in, and an hourly forecast from Open-Meteo for that site — no weather API key required.

On-site photos compress on the phone and land in IndexedDB the moment they are shot, even with no signal. A silent queue uploads them to the job (`/uploads/jobs`) as soon as the phone is back online. Uploaded shots attach to the active job and feed the measurement/estimate pipeline automatically — no tagging form, no “build from photos” step. With `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` or `OPENAI_API_KEY` the server reads the images; without a key, Job Command still prices a draft from the photo count, job notes, and Hot Springs paint rates. The job view only shows a compact photo count and a four-thumb strip.

Talk, type, or snap at the top of an open job. Speech-to-text and parsing run in the background and write scope, measurements, client asks, and draft estimate lines onto the job — no extra form. A lump-sum “materials $222” or a labor/total amount is enough to lock the bid; Job Command writes a General Materials / Scope (or labor/total) line if the itemized table is empty. The assistant talks like Job Command’s lead: a seasoned, rugged male general contractor — direct, sharp, focused on the job, crew, and estimates. The phone speaker scans Web Speech for Male / David / Mark / George / Guy, then speaks at pitch 0.7. Never “How may I help you today?”, never robotic pleasantries. Whisper runs when an AI key is set; without one, the phone transcript still parses locally.

**Send to client** is one tap on the finished estimate. Job Command emails and texts the phone link at the same time from whatever is on the client file (Resend + Twilio when those keys are set; otherwise it logs the messages locally). Delivery receipts (sent, delivered, opened, viewed) land in the background — no extra menus, no status table on the field screen. Open the Customer tab and expand the small send log to see when that estimate went out and when they opened it. Provider webhooks `/api/webhooks/resend` and `/api/webhooks/twilio` stay public so carriers can post receipts. Hourly cron `/api/cron/estimate-followup` nudges unopened estimates after 24 hours and unsigned ones after 48. Set `CRON_SECRET` in production.

**Invoice / Paid** compiles live labor hours and materials onto Dark Green, keeps every line editable until send, then texts/emails a Stripe card link (`/p/...`). The contractor’s linked Connect bank (Company → Link bank account, or `STRIPE_CONNECT_ACCOUNT_ID` as a fallback) receives 99%; Job Command keeps 1% as a Connect `application_fee`. Without `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` the same pay page still works locally. Stripe webhook `/api/webhooks/stripe` paints the bar solid when the charge clears, and also marks trial/subscription/addon/Connect status from Checkout and `customer.subscription.*` / `account.updated`.

New shops get a **30-day free trial** of the base Job Command plan ($199/mo flat-rate, or $1,990/yr billed yearly). Signup verifies that trial with Stripe (`trialing` or `active`), stores the Stripe customer id, keeps the Base App unlocked, and locks the **AI answering line** (+$59/mo) until that add-on is purchased. Confirmed shops land on the orange estimate stage; if Stripe is delayed or errors, Company billing prompts for a card without blocking the rest of the UI. After the trial, office cannot send estimates or invoices until a card is on file; crew clocks still work. Company billing opens Stripe Checkout (or a local mock when no secret key is set) and the Stripe customer portal for card changes. Year-end CSV/PDF packets use the same lightweight unlock — never a clock-in gate. Signup cannot finish until the owner checks **I agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy** (`/terms`, `/privacy`): 30-day Base App trial then $199/mo (or $1,990/yr) auto-renewal, AI answering +$59/mo, Stripe Connect merchant payouts with an automated 1% platform fee on client invoices, and a job-site liability limit (GPS, hours, estimates, and safety notes are operational aids, not a safety program). Stripe webhooks (`customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `invoice.payment_succeeded`), database access, fee application, and AI parsing stay on the server.

Clock-ins and material reimbursements are costed in the background against the estimate. Command never puts a spreadsheet on the phone — if hours or materials pass the bid, a small amber cue appears on the job card and co-pilot mentions it. Completed jobs feed actual hours and rates back into the on-site photo estimator. Hourly cron `/api/cron/job-cost` refreshes snapshots.

The app is offline-first on the phone. Active jobs, clients, schedules, and draft estimates cache in IndexedDB after the first load. Site photos compress locally first, then sit in the same queue as voice notes, clock punches, and send-to-client until the driveway has signal. When the connection comes back, Job Command flushes the queue in the background. A small status dot sits next to the shop lockup: green online, amber while syncing, dim when offline. The service worker caches the shop mark, avatars, and job photos — not the HTML document — so a rebuild cannot serve a stale shell against new scripts. Open the app once on shop wifi so those assets are cached; a first visit with zero signal cannot hydrate the board.

Approvals, packed stops, and mid-day dispatch land as a small count badge in the header. Tap it for a slide-out list. Urgent items (signed estimate, same-day route change) push immediately via the Web Push API (Chrome/Android uses FCM under that). Set `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` in production, or let the first boot generate keys. Optional `FCM_SERVER_KEY` covers older Chrome. Quiet hours (default 7pm–7am) hold opened-quote and other low-priority notes until evening or morning; they never hide a signed bid or a same-day dispatch. Hourly cron `/api/cron/alerts` releases held notes.

Office vs field is enforced on the server, not just the dock. Payroll, company, exports, and dispatch packing return a silent 403 for a crew session. Set `SESSION_SECRET` in production; local uses a built-in fallback. The session cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, and Secure on Vercel — never localStorage. API routes, PIN logins, and server actions are rate-limited per IP (429 + `Retry-After`). Stripe, AI, and the database stay in server functions.

Chrome and Safari cannot block OS screenshots. A Capacitor wrap (`native/android/MainActivity.java`) sets Android `FLAG_SECURE` so recordings and Recents go black; iOS shielding lives in `native/ios/ScreenShieldPlugin.swift`. See `native/README.md`.

Clients stay in a background data layer, not a CRM tab. Job Command indexes profiles, site addresses, estimates, photos, and time against the customer as work is saved. **New job** is an instant search: tap Harbor (or any filed client) to attach them in one hit. Call / Text / Email sit as a compact strip inside the open job, not on the daily tumbler.

Hours total for the current week or biweek and roll forward on their own. Hired date is when they started, not the paycheck calendar.

The look is charcoal plates (`#0D0D0D` / `#1E1E1E`) with aviation gold (`#C9A227`) on owner name, job arrows, completed-stage checks, and the next-action button. Client names stay high-contrast white. The current step pulses a gold glow. Job steps keep their own colors: red call, orange visit, yellow crew, green work, dark green pay, slate done. Four looks still live on signup and Company. Words stay simple. When a task is done, that bar gets a checkmark and the gold glow moves on.

Words on the first screens stay plain: Who is this, Your name, Step 1 of 6 · Call. The six tools and the dock do not change. To see signup as a brand-new download, run `node reset-setup.js` (or office can reopen it at `/?setup=1`).

Runtime faults (a thrown render, a dropped network, a blip talking to the database) recover in the background. The phone stays on the dark shell — no crash card, no stack dump. Diagnostics post to `/api/diagnostics`. `/api/health` reports whether the database answered.
